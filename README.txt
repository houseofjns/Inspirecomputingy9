6. HOW TO STUDY
- Choose a unit from the dashboard or sidebar.
- Work through the 15 sections: overview, objectives, detailed bilingual notes, topic explanations, definitions, examples, tables/diagrams, instructions, practical activities, worked examples, exam points, glossary, slide outline, quiz and examination.
- Use the global search or unit glossary to find terms.
- Worksheet answers are available from the Worksheet Answers navigation item and within Worked Examples.

7. QUIZZES AND EXAMINATIONS
Each unit includes a practice quiz and an independent 60-minute examination with 84 questions for 100 marks:
Section A: 30 multiple-choice questions / 30 marks
Section B: 20 fill-in questions / 20 marks
Section C: 20 matching questions / 20 marks
Section D: 10 short responses / 10 marks
Section E: 4 essays / 20 marks
Students move forward only. The paper submits at the end of the timer or after the last question. Results show section totals and question-by-question review. Essay keyword marks are provisional and should be reviewed by a teacher.
Browser-only fullscreen and navigation warnings are deterrents, not tamper-proof exam security. Browsers may block fullscreen or page-lock behaviour.

8. OFFLINE AND DEPLOYMENT
For local use, extract the ZIP and open index.html. For best offline cache behaviour, serve the folder from a static local web server or deploy it to GitHub Pages/another static HTTPS host. On first load while online, the service worker caches the core files; later visits can use the cached portal. No paid dependency is required.

9. QUALITY CHECKS
The ZIP is built without the source PDFs or scanned page images. The HTML, CSS and JavaScript files are syntax-checked, the six curriculum units and 49 lesson groups are counted, each unit has at least 30 glossary terms for its 84-question exam generator, and the exam section counts total 84 questions / 100 marks. This build has not been fully click-tested in every browser/device.
